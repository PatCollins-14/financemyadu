This is an example for thinking, not a loan offer. No rate, payment or approval is stated or implied. Every figure beyond the two starting numbers below is left for you and a lender to fill in. Rental income is not guaranteed and may not count toward qualification under every program.

The example's starting numbers

  • Home value: $1.2 million (an estimate; a lender confirms value with an appraisal or automated valuation).
  • Project cost: $200,000 (an example; your builder's quote sets the real number).

Four steps

Step 1: Find your equity

Equity is the home's value minus what you owe. Our guide notes that the appraised value, minus any outstanding mortgage balance, sets the equity available. So the key unknown is your mortgage balance.

Step 2: See what each lender will allow

Every lender sets its own limits on how much of your home's value it will lend against. Those limits and your credit, income and property decide the real number. Ask each lender to show you theirs.

Step 3: Compare the paths

Use the checklist below, with your own numbers, to compare each path. Ask each lender for a Loan Estimate so you can compare the same things.

Step 4: Plan for the budget, not only the loan

Think about what the payment would do to your monthly budget with and without any rent from the ADU, and what it would mean for your current mortgage.

Who this fits

  • Homeowners with a substantial amount of equity who want to learn how a project this size is usually financed.

Who this does not fit

  • Homeowners who need an answer about their own approval. This page cannot give one.

Trade-offs

A larger project means a larger loan or a larger draw on your equity. That raises the stakes on the same trade-offs described on the other pages: your home is the collateral, there are closing costs, and your monthly budget changes.

What to ask a lender

  • Based on my balance and credit, what range of financing could be available for this project?
  • Which option fits my goal and why?
  • What would I need to provide to move forward?
