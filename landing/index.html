What it is

A HELOC based on After‑Renovation Value (ARV)

You borrow against your home's after-renovation value (ARV): what the appraiser says it will be worth once the ADU or renovation is done.

  • Second lien. It sits behind your first mortgage. No refinance.
  • A line of credit. It works like the HELOCs you've heard of.
  • Interest-only payments for the 10‑year draw period, then a 20‑year repayment period. Your payment stays low while the ADU is under construction, before any rent from it comes in.
  • No standard draws. No draw-by-draw inspections and no construction consultant.
  • One final inspection to confirm the work is done, with project supervision after closing.

Standard HELOC

Today's value × the lender's limit (80% to 85% in these examples), minus your first mortgage.

After‑Renovation (ARV) HELOC

The lesser of 95% of the finished value or 125% of today's value, minus your first mortgage.

Straight answers

Questions people ask first

Will I lose my first mortgage?

No. This is a second loan that sits behind your first mortgage. There's no refinance, so your first mortgage, its rate and its payment stay exactly as they are.

How is this different from a regular HELOC?

A regular HELOC is based on what your home is worth today. This one is based on what your home will be worth after the project is finished. To get there, it adds two things: a review of the project, and an appraisal of your home "as finished."

What happens when I click Get Pre‑qualified?

It opens our secure online application with E Mortgage Capital. Fill it out and your ADU loan advisor follows up to go over your home, your project and your options. Starting an application is not a loan approval.

Can I use my own contractor?

Yes. Your contractor gets reviewed: their license and insurance, a short questionnaire, the cost estimate and the plans. If they check out, they build it.

Does it work for a remodel without an ADU?

Yes. Kitchens, bathrooms, additions, interior and exterior remodels, roofing, siding, decks, porches and pools all qualify, on their own or alongside an ADU.

Are there draw inspections or a construction consultant?

No. There's no bank draw schedule and no HUD consultant, so your contractor works at cash-buyer speed instead of waiting on draw approvals. After closing, project oversight is streamlined, and one final 1004D completion inspection confirms the work is done. Your advisor reviews all rates, terms and costs with you transparently, up front.

What are the payments like while the ADU is being built?

Interest-only during the 10‑year draw period, so the payment stays low while the ADU is under construction and before any rent from it comes in. After that, the balance is repaid over 20 years. Interest-only payments don't pay down the balance, and the payment goes up when repayment starts. Your advisor shows you the real numbers for your line before you decide.

I'm buying a home. Can I use this right after closing?

Yes. There's no waiting period after the purchase. Buy the home, then set up the line based on its finished value and start the project.

Can I use it on a rental property?

No. This program is for primary homes and second homes, on single-family or two‑unit properties. If yours is a rental, your advisor can walk you through other options.

Building an ADU to rent out? Will that rent help me qualify?

Plan on qualifying with the income you have today, not on future rent from the new ADU. Rental income is not guaranteed and may not count toward qualification under every program. Your advisor will tell you how your situation is treated.

What about the rate and costs?

Those depend on your credit, your home and the size of the line, so they're covered one on one. Your advisor lays them out before you decide anything.
