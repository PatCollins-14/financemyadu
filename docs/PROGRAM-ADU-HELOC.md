Supplied by Pat in chat, 2026-10-31. Apply link on the funnel: the "Apply" button on the first option card (Aven Foundry flow).
This sheet holds the full program details and records where each one is used and where it is deliberately not used. It is guidance, not legal advice; E Mortgage Capital compliance has the final say.

## Kept off the page on purpose, and why

| Detail | Why it stays off |
|---|---|
| $5K to $1M line size, "$2M coming soon" | Dollar amounts and loan amounts are blocked on every page (CLAUDE.md rule 5, preflight `dollar-amount`). "Coming soon" is a future promise that is not yet true. |
| 89% / 75% CLTV, 55% / 50% DTI | Percentages are blocked (preflight `percent`); limits change and invite "how much can I get" offers that need disclosures. |
| 620 minimum credit score | Credit-score minimums were deliberately not copied from the live site either (COMPLIANCE-RULES.md, Appendix G); they change by investor. |
| 5-year draw, 15- or 30-year repayment | Draw and repayment periods are HELOC "payment terms" under Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026.16(d)). Stating them triggers required disclosures (fees, APR, maximum APR). |
| Fixed-rate options | Not a trigger term by itself, but any rate talk invites rate questions the page cannot answer; left for the conversation. |
| "Fast", "speed", "higher leverage" | Speed claims were already flagged for compliance; "higher leverage" pushes borrowing more. The page says "Apply online" and "no in-person appraisal" instead. |
| Positive cash flow requirement | Underwriting detail; better explained one to one than in an ad. |

To show any number later, compliance must approve it, with the required Regulation Z disclosures, on that page (`allowTerms` exception).

## Where the numbers do belong: the conversation

Use them in one-to-one conversations with someone who already asked (call, booked meeting, reply to their inquiry), not in ads, posts or mass emails. Quick pre-screen for the first call:

1. Primary home or investment property? (sets the CLTV and DTI limits)
2. Rough credit range: at or above 620?
3. Home value and current mortgage balance: is there enough equity, with $30K left after the line?
4. How much they need, and is it at least $5K and within $1M?
5. Income type and documents: W-2, 1099, tax returns or transcripts, or assets?
6. Monthly debts: does the payment keep them within the DTI limit and cash-flow positive?
7. Do they need the full amount now? (full initial draw is required)

If any answer is a clear no, compare the cash-out refinance or a renovation loan instead.
